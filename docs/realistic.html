Realistic

Get the scan in clean, keep its roughness variance, and let the terrain place its own detail. Everything on Building a world still applies; this is what changes.

The five moves

  1. Repack before importing anything. Every scan pack has AO in it, and AO here darkens twice.
  2. Density before art. A checker on the mesh settles the UV argument in ten seconds.
  3. Keep the roughness range. Variance is what makes a surface look real. Do not clamp it.
  4. Place detail from the world. Moss, slope rock and puddles come from signals the material already has.
  5. Spend parallax once. Occlusion is the only feature in either master that costs a sampler.

Repack the scan

Scan and library packs ship ORM, MRAO or RMA. All of them put ambient occlusion in a channel this master does not want. Mat → Remap Texture Channels… takes any of them to CRM.

Surface _CRMcavityroughnessmetallic
Landscape _HRCheightroughnesscavity
Foliage _CRTcavityroughnesstransmission
The renderer supplies its own occlusion. A baked AO map on top of it darkens twice, and the result is a scene that looks dirty rather than shaded. Cavity takes that slot: it multiplies base colour and specular, which is what micro shadowing actually does, and never reaches the AO pin.

The source AO map is thrown away, not converted. Cavity is its own bake off the same high poly - tight creases and edge curvature rather than broad occlusion - so use the pack's cavity or curvature output if it has one. Failing that, a high pass of the height map stands in.

Density before art

Put a checker on the mesh with the intended UVScale and look at it next to a mesh you already trust. Realistic art lives or dies on this and no amount of shader work fixes it later.

Hero props, doors, weapons1024 px/m
Walls, floors, rocks512 px/m
Terrain layers256 px/m

TriplanarScale is 1 ÷ tile size in cm, so a 4 m tile is 0.0025. On the landscape, run Fit UV Scale To Landscape… and give it metres.

Keep the roughness

Roughness variance is most of what separates a photographed surface from a painted one. Leave the scan's range alone and only clamp what the anti-aliasing forces you to.

RoughnessMin / RoughnessMax0 / 1 - as authored
Specular0.5
CavityContrast1.0
DistanceRoughnessFloor0.35
DistanceNormalFlatten0.55
Those last two are not optional on FXAA. Sub-pixel specular crawls without a temporal filter, and the material is the only place it can be removed. Push them up until distant surfaces stop sparkling in motion, not in a screenshot.

Blend subtly

Real materials meet along their own detail, but they do not announce it. Half the interlock a stylized scene wants.

BlendHeightAmount / BlendContrast0.45 / 0.5
BlendHeightAmount / BlendHeightContrast (landscape)0.35 / 0.5
Variation_HueRange4 degrees
Variation_SaturationRange / ValueRange0.06 / 0.12 - value over hue, the opposite of stylized

Let the terrain place its own detail

Three systems put material where material goes, from data the shader already has. None of them needs painting and none costs a texture sample.

Slope rock

Name a recipe layer Rock and it overlays wherever the ground gets steep.

SlopeRock_SlopeStart / SlopeEnd32 / 55 degrees
SlopeRock_HeightAmount / HeightContrast0.5 / 0.5
SlopeRock_NoiseAmount0.15 - enough that the boundary is not a contour line

Moss

Four weighted signals: crevices, slope, faces turned away from the sun, and noise. Start with amount and leave the rest.

Moss_Amount0.35
Moss_CavityAmount / CavityContrast1 / 1.5 - moss in the crevices
Moss_ShadeAmount / ShadeSoftness1 / 0.5 - moss off the sun
Moss_NoiseAmount / NoiseContrast1 / 2
SunDirectionmust match your directional light, or shade lands on the wrong faces

Puddles

Water gathers by porosity, which comes from cavity, and stands only on surfaces facing up. There is no puddle mask to paint.

Wetness_Darkening0.55
Wetness_RoughnessTarget0.25
Wetness_PorosityAmount1
Wetness_PuddleDepth0.15-0.25
Wetness_PuddleFacing0.8
bRippleson, with a real ripple normal. The generic noise default reads as glass

Spend parallax once

Cheap offset is one multiply and an add, and it sells brick, cobbles and plank floors at a grazing angle. Occlusion walks the height field and is the only thing in either master that spends a sampler slot, because a raymarch cannot be expressed as graph taps.

LayerN_Parallaxthe cheap one. Use it freely
LayerN_ParallaxOcclusionone hero surface. ParallaxAmount 0.04, ParallaxSteps 16

Checklist

  • Every incoming pack went through Remap Texture Channels, and no AO map is assigned anywhere.
  • Texel density was checked with a checker, not by eye on the final art.
  • No layer clamps its roughness range.
  • Slope rock, moss and puddles are placed by the material, not painted.
  • SunDirection matches the directional light.
  • Parallax occlusion appears on at most one surface in the scene.
  • Nothing sparkles in motion at ES3.1 with FXAA on.
